# Integrations

Operational locally: PostgreSQL/PostGIS through Prisma's official JS adapter; real web-to-API requests; durable in-app notifications and messages; versioned advisory provider ranking; tenant-scoped procurement and reporting; PDF rendering; TOTP authenticator validation. Redis service health is verified, but external job delivery is not.

Payments, object uploads, email/SMS/push, social authentication, live maps/location, external AI and enterprise SSO are absent or disabled. See EXTERNAL_DEPENDENCIES.md for prerequisites. No synthetic data is presented as live tracking or financial settlement.
