## External prerequisites

Payments require an approved South African marketplace/payout provider and its real sandbox credentials/capabilities. Email/SMS/push, maps, private S3-compatible storage, OAuth/SSO and LLM services also require actual provisioning. No placeholder marks any of them successful. Enter credentials through secure environment settings, never chat or source control.
