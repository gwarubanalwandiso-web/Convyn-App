# User roles

CUSTOMER may publish/list own requests, view own bids, accept quotes, view bookings, confirm completion and review. PROVIDER may submit verification information, go online after approval, view eligible opportunities, bid, and fulfil assigned jobs. ADMIN cannot self-register; bootstrap securely through the CLI, then sign in with a password and TOTP. Administrators review verification and inspect audit records. All permissions are enforced server-side and tested against cross-account requests.

Business/enterprise organisation membership is separate from platform roles. OWNER/ADMIN manage structure and employees; only OWNER may grant elevated organisation roles. OWNER/ADMIN/APPROVER decide PENDING procurement approvals and access reports; MEMBER can submit requests but cannot approve or change organisation structure. Customer accounts may belong to multiple isolated organisations. Only requests within the approved budget can book a provider.
